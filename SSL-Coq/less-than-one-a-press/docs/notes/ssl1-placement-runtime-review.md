# Chosen enemy positions: what the real JP update does

This finite transfer check grants one initialized stock actor's X/Y/Z and one synchronized Mario contact pose. It does not grant a larger hitbox, tangibility, a new behavior, a changed home, RNG, a useful cached floor, or a clone construction. Wafel 0.8.5 executes the pinned JP DLL; the surrounding scene is restored after the checked 499-input prefix at capture poll 500. Each trial then advances three uninterrupted neutral, A-released updates, with no further patches.

The scene contains twelve actors matching the four reviewed bounce species. The bounded test selects the first eight pool slots: three Goombas, three Fly Guys, Klepto and one Pokey body part. There are 24 trials, using the same three Mario poses for each actor: `(-2400,768,-1024)`, `(-2400,1280,-1024)`, and `(-2200,768,-1024)`. The actor is put at Mario's X/Z and the immediately preceding positive binary32 Y. All its live size, tangibility, action, parent and home fields remain unchanged. Mario's movement, collision and display start synchronized; his supplied action is freefall, vertical speed -4, horizontal speed zero and depth zero. Those contact poses are conditional test inputs, not reached gameplay states.

The supported Y=1280 trials also encounter the real nearby geometry during subsequent action movement; all seven bounced first updates end at Y=1439.0001220703125. That height is not their bounce snap and is not evidence of a retained gap. The report records the action entry and end-update coordinates separately.

Two independent executions agree exactly on all reported fields except elapsed time. In-process total time, including game creation, capture validation, preparation and all 72 trial updates, is 0.8145907 seconds and 0.8891443 seconds. These timings describe this small diagnostic, not a search-throughput estimate. The raw report is 118,269 bytes; its independently compressed committed copy is 3,930 bytes.
